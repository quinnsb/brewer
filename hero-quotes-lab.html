    What to look at

    Reroll a few times and watch how the long ones wrap. Fifteen lines are in data/library-quotes.json; every one has a source so the attribution can be checked rather than trusted, because a lot of the quotations that circulate about art and books are misattributed. Anything that could not be tied to a real book, letter or interview was left out.

    The reveal is the same per-character mask the old verb swap used, so the hero still moves the way the rest of the page does. It does not cycle: one quote per visit. A verb rotating every couple of seconds is a texture, but a sentence swapping while you are half way through reading it is not.

    All lines shows every quote at once, which is the quicker way to spot the ones that wrap badly. Narrow and wide set the stage width without resizing the window.
