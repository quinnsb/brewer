Nine LinkedIn profile banners, drawn at 1584 by 396. That 4 to 1 letterbox is a different problem from the featured-link thumbnail. There is almost no vertical room, so each one is a single band of content, and the bottom left corner is unusable because your profile photo sits on top of it. Every layout here starts its content clear of that corner.

Turn on guides to see the three zones. They are approximate: LinkedIn does not publish where the photo lands and has moved it more than once, so treat them as a zone to design against and check one against your own profile before committing.

Copy bank

Aimed at marketing director roles, so the framing moved from what you make to what you run. Your three formulas are still here underneath: the first column is I help X do X with the verb raised to lead, the second is doing X powered by X with the outcome moved to the front, and the third pairs a principle with something a hiring committee can check. Any of these drops into A, B, C, G or H without touching the layout.

Lines marked in grey are yours or are backed by something already on the site. The rest are new positioning, so read them as claims you need to be willing to defend in an interview.

I lead X

  • I lead marketing teams and the systems they run on.
  • I build marketing functions, not just campaigns.
  • I lead brand, content, and campaign strategy end to end.
  • I lead the strategy, the team, and the work. your line, rebuilt for a title
  • I turn scattered marketing into one engine.
  • I lead marketing where strategy and craft are the same job.

Marketing that does X

  • Brand, content, and demand under one strategy.
  • Full-funnel marketing, from positioning to pipeline.
  • Marketing engines built to run without me.
  • Brand strategy that shows up in the numbers.
  • Positioning, campaigns, and the content engine behind them.
  • Marketing that compounds instead of resetting every quarter.

Proof and principle

  • Grew a nonprofit podcast past one million downloads. your strongest number
  • Leading marketing at an agency across a portfolio of brands. from your about page
  • Brand and content leadership for complex missions.
  • Strategy at the foundation. Results at the end of it. your line, given a business ending
  • I build systems other people can run.
  • From positioning to podcast to pipeline.

Retire these for a director search

Every line below is good writing and all of them are on your site, but each one signals maker rather than leader. On a portfolio that is the point. On a profile a hiring committee is scanning for a director, it reads as someone who wants to stay close to the craft, which is the exact objection you are trying to pre-empt.

  • I sweat the strategy. I make the thing. I answer the email. The third beat is the problem. It is charming and it says individual contributor.
  • Brands built by hand. Reads craftsperson. A director is hired to build the function, not the artifact.
  • Powered by one very involved human. Actively argues you are a team of one.
  • I help small teams punch above their weight. Service-provider framing. Puts you outside the org rather than running part of it.
  • Nothing gets passed down a chain. Directors are the chain. This one reads as anti-delegation.
